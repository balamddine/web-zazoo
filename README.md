# Web Zazoo

A browser virtual-pet game starring Zazoo, a fluffy teal monster.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (keep it next to the `sprites` folder), or enable **GitHub Pages** for this repository (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) and open the published URL.

## What Zazoo does

- **Eat:** pick a food (apple, carrot, cookie, pizza, cake). Healthy food lowers weight, junk food raises it.
- **Gets fat:** feeding him when he's already full makes him fat. He slims down again by digesting or exercising.
- **Exercise:** 6 jumps that burn weight and energy.
- **Kiss:** he blushes, with floating hearts.
- **Tap:** he laughs (or his belly jiggles when fat). Tap too fast and he gets upset.
- **Sleep:** lights dim and his energy refills. He also falls asleep by himself when tired.
- **Hunger alerts and nagging:** when he's hungry he sends a browser notification. If ignored, he shouts and nags.

The **Time** button speeds up time (×10, ×60) for testing.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game (HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step)
- `sprites/*.mp4`: the animations (960px, 25 fps, H.264)
